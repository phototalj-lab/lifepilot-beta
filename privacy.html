LifePilot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Local-first data

LifePilot stores tasks, settings, reminders, and backups locally on your device or browser storage. The current beta does not send your task data to a server.

Backups

When you export a backup, LifePilot creates a JSON file on your device. You control where that file is saved or shared.

Notifications

LifePilot can request notification permission to remind you about urgent local tasks. Notification permission can be changed in your browser or Android settings.

Contact

For beta support, use the contact channel shared with your LifePilot beta invite.
